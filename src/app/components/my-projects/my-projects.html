WEB APPLICATION - HCI Lab

BankBox — TD Banking Interface

Worked as a Research Assistant in the Human-Computer Interaction Lab at the University of Manitoba on BankBox, a research project designed to help users build confidence with online banking. I developed a simulated TD banking interface that allows users especially older adults to safely practice common tasks such as bill payments, e-transfers and many other features. It also includes guided learing , test modules and hint videos

02

WEB APPLICATION - Chatbot

Bisonbot

This was a project built for a hackathon at the University of Manitoba with a team of four people. My main responsibility was working on the frontend, where I focused on designing and developing the user interface and integrating it with the backend. The idea behind the project was to create a chatbot for U of M students who may feel uncomfortable reaching out to science advisors or who simply have quick questions that can be answered online. The chatbot was designed to provide students with an accessible and convenient way to find information and get answers to common questions without having to contact an advisor directly.

TODO APP

This was a simple project I built while learning Angular, and it was also my first experience working with databases. This project helped me gain hands-on experience with Angular, user authentication, CRUD operations, and connecting a frontend application to a database.
